Users must conduct their own independent due diligence or consult a licensed SEBI-registered financial planner before executing any financial transaction.

### 3. Machine Learning & Quantitative Signal Disclosures
All directional forecasts, regime classifications, and risk scorecards generated by the `ml_engine` and `ai_analysis` apps are algorithmic outputs derived from historical price and volume data:
- **Past Performance:** Historical patterns, backtest outcomes, and model scores do not guarantee or predict future market performance.
- **Probabilistic Nature:** Machine learning models identify statistical regularities. Market regimes can shift instantaneously due to unforeseen macroeconomic shocks, regulatory actions, geopolitical disruptions, or liquidity cascades.
- **Experimental Models:** Features labeled "Experimental" or "Shadow Pipeline" represent active empirical research and must not be used to drive capital allocation decisions.

### 4. Shadow Evaluation vs. Live Execution
The **Forward Shadow Pipeline** operates strictly out-of-sample:
- Shadow setups are logged chronologically and evaluated forward in time without retroactive adjustment.
- Transaction frictions (Securities Transaction Tax [STT], exchange turnover fees, GST, SEBI turnover charges, Stamp Duty, and 0.05% slippage) are calculated synthetically based on standard Indian equity cash delivery (CNC) schedules.
- Simulated returns do not account for liquidity constraints, circuit limit locks, corporate actions, or order execution delays experienced in real trading accounts.

### 5. Third-Party Market Data Feeds
Market prices, corporate financials, news feeds, and index values are fetched from external third-party providers (Yahoo Finance, NSE Public Data feeds). KuberLens and its developers make no warranties, express or implied, regarding the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or sequencing of any external market data.
