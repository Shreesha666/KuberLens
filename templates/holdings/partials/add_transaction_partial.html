How Portfolio Accounting Works
1. First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Lots

Every buy transaction creates an immutable tax lot. When you record a sell order, lots are relinquished in chronological order to compute exact realized gains.

2. Capitalized Acquisition Costs

Brokerage and statutory taxes (STT, stamp duty, GST) are automatically factored into your effective cost basis per share.

3. Corporate Actions Supported

Log stock splits, zero-cost bonus shares, and dividend cash payouts directly using the transaction type selector.
