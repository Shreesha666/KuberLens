Why Multi-PAN Family Bidding Matters Under SEBI Rules

Under SEBI's proportionate lottery system, applying for 10 lots from a single PAN in an oversubscribed IPO gives you the exact same odds (1 ticket) as applying for 1 lot. However, applying for 1 lot across 4 distinct family PANs quadruples your mathematical allotment probability.
